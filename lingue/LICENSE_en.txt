YAEC is free software, written by a radio amateur for radio amateurs. Its code was also developed with the help of an artificial intelligence assistant, and the licence follows its way of working: be really useful, always say how things are, show your own reasoning, harm no one and acknowledge the work of others. Below are the principles and then the actual conditions.

• Useful and free: The program is free and you can use it without limits for your amateur radio activity, for study and for teaching. It is meant to make QSOs happen, not to sell something.
• Transparent: The source is always delivered with the program; every formula is visible in the Calculation check tab and every data item gives its source. No black boxes.
• Honest: Measured, derived and estimated values are kept apart; what is not verified is declared as such. An uncertain result is shown as uncertain, not presented as certain.
• Respectful: It respects the work of those who measured, wrote and published before us, the privacy of its users (no data collected, no advertising) and the rules of the amateur radio service.
