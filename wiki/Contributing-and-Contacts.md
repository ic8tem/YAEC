## What to send

- **Errors in a calculation or in a data item.** Say which tab, band, locators and instant; a copy of the [Calculation check](Excel-Export-and-Calculation-Check) (*Copy to clipboard* or *Save as Word*) helps a lot. If you have a better value, include its source: it will be corrected, citing you.
- **Crashes.** Attach `errori.txt` from the [data folder](Settings-and-Data-Folder) (on macOS also `mono-log.txt`), your operating system and the YAEC version.
- **Translations.** The interface, the two manuals, the licence and the README exist in 36 languages; corrections from native speakers are very welcome. For the licence, the Italian text prevails in case of doubt.
- **New data.** Coaxial cables with data sheets, measured noise figures of radios, antennas, and more.
- **Transverters.** If you would like to make other transverters – or your own – known, write to the author: the data will be added to § 1.7.1 of THEORY AND TIPS ON EME, citing the source.
