## Principles

| | |
|---|---|
| **Useful and free** | Free to use without limits for amateur radio activity, study and teaching. It is meant to make QSOs happen, not to sell something. |
| **Transparent** | The source is always delivered with the program; every formula is visible in the Calculation check tab and every data item gives its source. No black boxes. |
| **Honest** | Measured, derived and estimated values are kept apart; what is not verified is declared as such. |
| **Respectful** | Of those who measured, wrote and published before us, of users' privacy (no data collected, no advertising) and of the rules of the amateur radio service. |

## Conditions in brief

1. **Use** – use, copy and install YAEC free of charge, on as many computers as you like.
2. **Changes** – study the source, fix and improve it for your own use or your radio club.
3. **Redistribution** – only together with its complete source and under this same licence.
4. **Declaring the changes** – a modified version must say so clearly (title or release notes), stating who modified it and what changed.
5. **Attribution** – keep the author's name (Costantino IC8TEM), the "Sources, data and credits" chapter and all third-party source citations.
6. **No commercial use** – selling YAEC or derived versions, or including it in paid products, is not allowed without the author's written permission.
7. **No hidden data** – no distributed version may collect user data, show advertising or send information to third parties without saying so openly.
8. **Proper use of the radio** – frequencies, powers and modes remain the responsibility of the operator and of his or her licence.
9. **No warranty** – provided as it is; calculations are made with care and declared sources but may contain errors: check important data before relying on them.
10. **Reports** – if you find an error or a better data item, write to the author: it will be corrected, citing you as the source.

> **In short:** use it freely, learn from the code, improve it and share it – but always with the source, with the sources cited, saying what you changed and without selling it. If you doubt a result, the right answer is to check, not to trust.
