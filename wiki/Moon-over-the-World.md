# Moon over the world

*Group: Planning.*

A **world map with the land coloured by the Moon's elevation** as seen from each place, at a chosen moment. The coloured areas are the places that can see the Moon at that moment; if the Moon is also above your horizon, they can see it at the same time as you – they are your potential EME partners. Seas are not coloured.

## What it shows

- the land coloured by Moon elevation, from the horizon (0°) to the zenith;
- the point where the Moon is at the **zenith** (El 90°), with the place it is over;
- your station and **your antenna status**: Moon pointable, outside your limits or below your horizon.

## Using it

| Action / control | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Hover the mouse** | Shows the Moon elevation, azimuth and distance from that place. |
| **Click** | Uses that point as the **DX station** (in all tabs). |
| **Calculation time** | Same moment as the [Moon position](Moon-Position) tab: if you change it here, it changes there too. |
| **Follow the current time** | Updates the map minute by minute. |

Your own locator is changed in the [Moon position](Moon-Position) tab (or on the main screen).

## Typical uses

- Quickly see which continents share the Moon with you right now.
- Pick a DX region for the next window and check how high the Moon is there.
- Check whether a planned DX will have the Moon at a reasonable elevation when it is pointable for you.
